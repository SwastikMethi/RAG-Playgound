# Starlight Queue release notes

This is a fictional document for RAG Playground development and testing.

## September 1 2026

Pro event retention increased from 7 days to 30 days, effective September 1, 2026. The event-rate and concurrent-consumer limits did not change.

Starter event retention remains 72 hours. Existing Pro workspaces receive the increased retention without changing their consumer configuration.

## August 1 2026

During August 2026, Pro event retention was 7 days. This earlier entry describes the policy before the September change.

The service added the NEBULA-42 diagnostic code for client clock offsets larger than 120 seconds.
