Status colors have accompanying labels and icons. Use locally available system sans-serif at 16px for answers, 14px for controls, and no essential metadata below 12px; tabular numerals for durations. The distinctive element is the connected evidence workflow and its synchronized stage/evidence inspection. No marketing hero, ornamental charts or score-as-confidence indicators.

Desktop: rail | question + two/three lanes | optional inspector.
